## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro

## Users

Experienced Mac users who want a weekly guide to thoughtfully selected apps, tools, and reading.

## Product Purpose

App Waypoint is a weekly editorial publication that helps Mac power users discover useful software and related reading. Success means publishing a focused Friday issue that feels trustworthy, timely, and worth returning to.

## Positioning

The product’s edge is human-curated selection for experienced Mac users, with AI used as editorial support rather than as the final decision-maker.

## Operating Context

The editorial workflow runs on a weekly cadence. Every Friday, the team researches candidates from community discussion, product launches, developer releases, podcasts, videos, and official product information, then publishes one issue.

Issues currently include app recommendations, a video recommendation, and weekend reading.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site presents a curated issue, app archive, tag pages, collection pages, an about page, and issue-specific reading content.

Editorial constraints from the README include:

- prioritize usefulness, community excitement, polish, active development, and unique functionality;
- avoid repeating the same app unless there is a major update, redesign, sale, feature launch, or renewed community interest;
- include Deals & Bundles only when they are genuinely worthwhile;
- include actual articles in Weekend Reading, not software recommendations;
- keep final selections, editorial emphasis, and recommendations human-led.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is App Waypoint.

The current voice is editorial, practical, and selective rather than promotional.

Confirmed brand assets include the App Waypoint logo and the existing visual system used by the current site.

## Product Principles

- Curate for usefulness first, not novelty for its own sake.
- Keep the editorial process human-led, even when AI helps with research and organization.
- Favor consistency and repeatability in the weekly issue format.
- Serve experienced Mac users with software that fits real workflows.
